ImageConverter – Single Line

A web tool that turns any JPG or PNG image into one continuous line drawing and exports it as G-code, ready for pen plotters, CNC machines or engravers.

  • Two drawing modes: TSP art (dots connected into a single path) and a spiral radiating from the center
  • Adjustable point density, output size in millimeters, pen up/down commands and drawing speed
  • Live preview of the generated path with zoom and pan
